# iOS Release

- App name: HoCo Weather Channel
- Subtitle: School weather for Maryland
- Bundle identifier: `com.hocoweatherchannel.app`
- Support URL: `https://hocoweatherchannel.org/mobile-support`
- Privacy policy URL: `https://hocoweatherchannel.org/mobile-support#privacy`

## Store Description Draft

See what local weather could mean for the school day. HoCo Weather Channel brings together Howard County forecasts, hourly conditions, school operation predictions, and reported district status from across Maryland.

See the reasons behind a HoCo prediction and check official district announcements separately. HoCo Weather Channel predictions are not official school district decisions.

## Before Submission

1. Confirm the Apple Developer account holder and seller name, and connect an Expo account to the project.
2. Confirm the support and privacy URLs are live. Review the App Privacy answers against actual app and hosting behavior.
3. Build the production iOS app with Xcode 26 or later and an iOS 26 SDK, then upload the signed build to App Store Connect.
4. Test through TestFlight on iPhone and iPad. Capture store screenshots from the tested build and complete the age-rating and review forms before submitting.

The website's Route Weather map is not part of the native app build.
